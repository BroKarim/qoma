## a-soft-panel

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a polished visual design draft of ONE compact floating countdown timer for the qyube macOS focus app. Reference image 1 is the accepted Collector's Desk app design: borrow its white/light neutral grey materials, dark ink, delicate outlines and subtle tactile inset depth. Reference image 2 is the current timer: preserve its compact footprint, approximate width-to-height ratio 1.8:1, and sole information content. The timer must display ONLY the large centered text "24:53". No other text, icons, labels, penguins, fish, illustrations, buttons, progress bars, decorations, mouse cursor, windows or app chrome. This is the idle unhovered appearance. Numerals must be clear at a real widget size around 220x120 pixels. Use refined moderately weighted elegant serif numerals related to the metric numerals in reference 1, highly legible, not ornate or thin hairlines. Front-on flat UI screenshot, no perspective, no physical product photography. Present a single timer centered on a uniform medium neutral grey presentation background, timer occupying about half the image width with generous surrounding margin. Landscape canvas. Timer face white to very light neutral grey, text near black. Subtle light upper rim, fine grey outline, short soft shadow, shallow layered depth just like reference 1, no glossy plastic or chrome, no dramatic gradients. No duplicated sizes, comparison sheets, captions or watermark.
Variant A SOFT PANEL: compact rounded rectangle, generous continuous 26px-like corners. Fine outer border and one very subtle inset rounded rectangle forming a shallow collector tray. Smooth clean white surface, beautifully restrained dual rim and shadow. Shape close to original timer but visually belongs to reference 1.
