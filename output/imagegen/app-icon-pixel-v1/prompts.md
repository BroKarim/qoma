# Qyube pixel app icon drafts

Built-in imagegen. Three independent drafts. User icon collage used as conceptual guidance for simple mascot silhouettes and bold backgrounds. Not installed into AppIcon. Export sizing and small-size QA remain for the selected direction.

## a-penguin-face
+
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: high quality macOS app icon draft for qyube, a cozy penguin Pomodoro focus and fishing collectible app. ONE large square icon centered and filling the canvas, rounded-square app icon tile, no text, no letters, no mockup device, no captions, no watermark. Original design, strong simple silhouette readable at 32px. Strict beautiful pixel art: crisp square pixel clusters, consistent pixel grid, carefully controlled stepped edges, restrained 3 shade ramps, no watercolor, no blurry rendering, no vector smooth character. Charming premium indie-game identity. Arctic porcelain, ink navy, icy cyan with tiny warm ochre accents. A bold close-up chubby baby penguin face and upper body, front facing, charcoal navy head, generous white cheeks and belly, two small friendly black pixel eyes, tiny golden beak, tiny flippers. Character occupies 76 percent tile. Plain muted ice-blue tile with subtle pixel edge shading. Most minimal and mascot-driven, iconic and memorable.
