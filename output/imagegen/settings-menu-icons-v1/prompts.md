## Selected direction and production prompt — 24 October 2026

Concept A was selected. The six production icons were generated as separate transparent images so each can be bundled and scaled independently. Shared prompt guidance:

> Create one single standalone hand-drawn watercolor illustration of **[subject]** for a cozy macOS app menu. Use the selected Concept A visual language: delicate irregular dark graphite ink contours, visible watercolor pigment and restrained paper grain within the painted object, porcelain/ice-blue/fjord palette (#FAFBF8, #E4EFF2, #386779, #253E4A) with a tiny ochre detail where appropriate. Keep the silhouette simple and immediately recognizable at 24–32pt. Center the complete object with generous transparent padding. Transparent background (alpha), no scene, no tile, no label, no letters or extra props, no shadow outside the object, no glossy 3D, no vector-flat finish. Match the other five icons in scale, line weight, viewpoint, pigment density, and warmth.

Subjects used: Settings — pale ice-blue cogwheel with a dark center; Manage subscription — blue membership card with a small ochre star seal and no text; Sign out — open doorway with a clear outward arrow; Help — compact blue book with a question mark; Give feedback — blue speech bubble with a small pencil; Quit — clean rounded fjord-blue power symbol. The final icons are in `Qoma/Assets.xcassets/QyubeMenu*.imageset` and are downsampled for menu use.

Generation caveat: these were generated independently, so exact watercolor texture and line contours vary slightly. Keep the shared prompt and inspect all six together at their in-app size when generating future revisions.
