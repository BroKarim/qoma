# Aquarium macOS concept drafts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Static exploratory mockups, not implemented UI. Some fish/submarine silhouettes use oblique angles; production assets should use strict top-down. Counts and populations are illustrative.

## 01-ice-pool

Use case: ui-mockup. Generate one polished high-fidelity macOS desktop app screenshot concept for Dzenn's Aquarium page. Wide landscape 3:2 composition, entire straight-on app window visible, native red yellow green window buttons, no perspective device mockup. This is a cozy focus timer collection app. White/ivory background and black charcoal typography and controls, penguin-inspired palette with ice blue water and tiny muted orange accents. Left sidebar about 18% width: small original penguin mark and "Dzenn", navigation "Focus", "Aquarium" selected, "Catalog", "Analytics", and "Settings" near bottom. Main header "Aquarium", subtle "18 discoveries", compact biome selector "Calm Sea" and small "Catalog" button. Aquarium dominates at least 75% of main content. Camera for everything IN the aquarium is strict overhead top-down, fish backs visible, no side-view tank, no glass front, no isometric view. About 14 varied individually recognizable small illustrated collectible entities: fish with flexible tails, ray, tiny shark, miniature submarine viewed from above, seaweed clusters, shell, starfish, small lost bottle. Airy spacing, each species once, lots of swim room. Muted colorful fish can have black white gray bodies and restrained orange patches. Refined native desktop typography, readable labels, calm uncluttered layout, no charts, no dashboard card clutter, no editing/decorating controls, no big penguin character in tank, no photorealism, no watermark.
DRAFT 1 ICE POOL. Main content presents a large horizontal rounded-rectangle ice-blue pool with subtle frosted white rim, gently rounded organic corners, translucent very pale turquoise water, softly visible square bottom tiles and delicate caustic ripples. Contemporary crisp flat illustration with subtle dimensional shadows beneath fish. Sidebar selected row black with white icon/text. Spacious beautiful Apple-like product design. Small unobtrusive bottom caption "Your focus, brought to life." Understated view-control icon in pool corner. Palette 80% white across interface; pool pale blue. Make the pool expansive and the marine items charming and distinct.
